It is a lead with a hard evidentiary core and a large speculative surround, and the two are kept
apart below on purpose.

## Who Valerijs Černohajev is

A real, public, documented figure — not a private individual, which is why this section exists at all.

- Soviet aerospace engineer, 1958–2019. Born Guryev, Kazakh SSR; Riga Civil Aviation Engineering
  Institute, graduated 1981; died 30 December 2019 in Kazakhstan.
- Left ~119 pages of manuscripts (dated 1980–2007) describing what he called reverse-engineered
  "UFO" propulsion, power generation, hull materials, and a framework he named gravitational-charge
  dualism.
- Inherited by his daughter, **Natalja Černohajeva-Sticco** (Riga-born mezzo-soprano and IT
  engineer). Translated by her, **Gene Sticco** (retired USAF, founder of ASIRP), **Mario Fialho**
  (AI solutions architect), and **Dr. Andis Dembovskis** (Latvian, PhD Bremen, space
  communications). Published 2024 as *Engineering Infinity: Earth's First Interstellar Blueprint*.
- Public footprint: `engineeringinfinitybook.com`, the Omeka archive at `cernohajev.omeka.net`
  (operated by "The Černohajev Archive and Research Institute, a subsidiary of Blackgrove Global
  Risk"; its API is disabled), a documentary *Infinity Disclosed*, a NewsNation *Reality Check*
  segment, and a press conference on **2025-06-11** at the Malden Irish American Club, Malden, MA,
  with a MUFON Massachusetts field investigator as guest.

## Why it matters: the connection exists nowhere else

Searched thoroughly, both directions:

- No public source links Černohajev or *Engineering Infinity* to Skinny Bob, ivan0135, or qtecqot.
- `@InfinityDisclsd` (Gene Sticco), `@DirtyWaterDiva` (Natalja Sticco), and every associated account
  found: **zero** posts mentioning Skinny Bob, ivan0135, or qtecqot.
- The archive's own pages carry no mention of film, tape, footage, an extraterrestrial being, the
  KGB, or the 1942–1969 range the videos claim.

So the only artifact in existence joining these two bodies of material is a tweet qtecqot wrote and
then deleted.

## The @UAPJedi coincidence

qtecqot follows exactly three accounts. One is **`@UAPJedi`** (dossier §1; the follow drove
FINDINGS §6e.1). On **2026-02-26**, two months before qtecqot's account existed, `@UAPJedi` posted a
12-part thread — explicitly a Grok-generated synthesis of a Gene Sticco Substack article — whose
part 4/12 opens *"Enter the Soviet key: Valerijs Černohajev (1958–2019), aerospace engineer."*

Constraints on reading this:

- `@UAPJedi` has **never** posted about Skinny Bob, ivan0135, or qtecqot. There is **no public
  interaction in either direction** between the two accounts.
- The thread parts carry no original media. The one photo in the root is a quoted `@InfinityDisclsd`
  post, 1080×1920 JPEG — **not** a match for the 595×449 PNG.
- Following lists are not queryable logged out, so the *order* (did he follow @UAPJedi before or
  after seeing the thread?) is not established.

What it does establish is availability: the Černohajev material was in front of the one UFO account
qtecqot follows, in the window before he registered, and he then named it and deleted it.

## What this does and does not support

**Supported:** the operator knew of the Černohajev archive before launching, considered it relevant
enough to name in his own opening thread with an image, and removed it at the moment of going
public. Under any provenance hypothesis, that is a deliberate editorial act.

**Not supported:** that the archive is a *source* for the videos. No content comparison has been
done. That is the next piece of work, and it is genuinely testable — see below.

**Explicitly unresolved:** what the 595×449 image showed. A page from a manuscript, a portrait, a
screenshot of the archive site, and a screenshot of someone else's post are all consistent with the
size and format, and nothing in hand discriminates between them.

would assert a link the evidence does not carry. Under `AGENTS.md`, the two public creators
(ivan0135, qtecqot) remain the whole exception. This file stays internal until, at minimum, step 1
returns something.
