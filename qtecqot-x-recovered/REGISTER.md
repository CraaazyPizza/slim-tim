# The register: what vocabulary the account reaches for

Written 2026-08-02, prompted by a simple observation: `UNK cond.` is not a phrase most people
have in their fingers. Once the deleted posts were recovered there was enough text to check
whether that is a one-off or a pattern.

**Corpus:** 19 X posts, **584 words** total, plus the video 1 title card and two video titles.
That is small. Everything below is a description of the corpus, not a claim about a person.

## The markers

| in the corpus | register it belongs to |
|---|---|
| `UNK cond.` | casualty and personnel status reporting: *unknown condition* |
| `ivan compromised` | intelligence sense of compromised, an asset or channel, not an emotion |
| `Incapacitation presumed` (video 1 title card) | status reporting, hedged assertion of a person's state |
| `release 5 -8 is triggered` | of a pre-set condition firing, not of an emotional reaction |
| `DMS`, expanded a day later to `Deadman's Switch` | abbreviation used first, glossed only when asked |
| `I have been given access to less than 2% of the network's cache of materials, with authorization to distribute` | holdings and handling register: access, cache, authorization to distribute |
| `intermittent contact through electronic means as circumstances dictated` | contact reporting, and near-verbatim after-action phrasing |
| `ET crew recovery site D` | recovery sites designated by letter |
| `EBL Tim +2`, `Exit EBL04` | entity designator plus number, plus a casualty-count idiom (`+2`) |
| `Any censorship or blackout should be presumed as government/corporate intervention` | hedged status assertion again: *should be presumed* |

Fourteen distinct markers in 584 words. In a corpus that short, that density is the finding.

## What it does and does not tell you

**It is consistent with** somebody who has worked around military, intelligence, emergency
services, or aviation status reporting, in any capacity, in any country. It is equally
consistent with somebody who reads a lot of that material, and the SERPO corpus he names in his
first post is written throughout in exactly this register: `CAC`, `CO`, `FAC`, `NOC`, `PG`,
`GURVO`, `GUCOS`, `Second Chief Directorate`, glossaries at the head of releases. **A person
steeped in that source text would produce this vocabulary without ever having held a clearance.**
See `SERPO_LEAD.md`.

**It is not a nationality claim and not an occupation claim.** 584 words cannot carry either.
The repo's own standing warning applies with full force: confidence here was inversely
correlated with correctness on far larger evidence than this.

## What cuts against a "trained writer" reading

- `There is- an error in the tables on pages 3, 6 and 7.` — the stray hyphen is not a typo an
  edited register produces.
- `I am not Ivan0135. I have not met him/her` — the `him/her` is careful in a civilian way.
- `Any "ads" associated with the YouTube posts were not at my direction. If anyone knows how to
  completely disable this feature, please advise me.` — the *content* is someone unfamiliar with
  YouTube's monetization settings, while `not at my direction` and `please advise me` are the
  formal register again. Those two things sitting in one post is the most interesting sentence
  in the corpus.
- The one glaring misspelling anyone has attributed to him, `THERE TRYING TOO SILENCE ME`, is
  **not his** — it is the copycat channel's text, quoted in a post calling that channel fake.
  Recorded here so it does not get laundered into a stylometry claim later.
