## Historical branding is not an identity

The [documented Facebook page history](qtecqot-facebook-zzuss-2026-09-08.md) connects the same page ID from Zzuss.com to DeTomaso.org through a March 2024 rename. The archived shopping and registry sites also share code. These are useful project-history observations, but do not establish that a single individual controlled them throughout.

The [domain inspection](qtecqot-domain-2026-09-08.md) records a current May 2026 registration event for detomaso.org. Historical content cannot establish uninterrupted ownership across that event. Hosting country, a support mailbox name, and a car-registry description do not establish a person's location, occupation or identity.

## What would establish more

A website-linked business notice or an explicit statement from an established site account could identify the publicly declared operator. A response describing its role would initially establish a claim, not independently authenticate a connection to the video uploader. A reciprocal acknowledgment from qtecqot's established account would be stronger evidence of affiliation.

The current operator and any affiliation with the video uploader remain unresolved.
