# Audit of the CARI / Blackgrove / ASIRP provenance arguments

**Scope.** The manuscript corpus attributed to V.A. Černohajev, its twelve critical editions, two
derived preprints, and the analytical and promotional documents built around them, as held at
`/tmp/claude-1001/-home-user-new-skinny-bob/0a8dd3d2-3fd5-4e22-b0d7-a55e36ce5c1c/scratchpad/ei/`
(`txt/` extractions, `reports/` source PDFs, `scans/` and `small/` page images).

**Standing constraint.** Provenance is undetermined and this audit does not settle it. What follows
characterises how much weight the *arguments* can carry, not whether the manuscripts are what they
are said to be. "AI-generated" appears nowhere below as a conclusion. No private individual is
named; where personal data exists, only its type and location are recorded.

**Method.** I read the Period Plausibility Analysis, the ACH, the Rosetta Stone, the podcast kit
and the clip scripts directly, verified the arithmetic of the corpus's central derivation, and
examined the page facsimiles. Four parallel sweeps covered the twelve critical editions, the two
preprints, the Dossier, the evidence map, the Biographical Foundation, the complexity paper and the
Pais-patents breakdown, each required to separate the **manuscript layer** (Russian transcription +
English translation) from the **editor's layer** (notes, reconstruction, commentary). A 2024–26
editor may cite 2020s work; only a modern value in the author's own voice counts as a hit.

---

## Summary of the four things that matter most

1. **The anachronism check comes back clean on the constants, and the null is unusually strong** —
   not merely "nothing found" but a coherent pre-1978 planetary data set whose *errors propagate
   correctly*. Details and detection floors in §3.
2. **One manuscript page self-dates to 2024**, outside both claimed composition windows (§3.6).
3. **The archive's most probative potential artifact — a dated 2012 photographic posting — is
   quoted at length by one document that simultaneously says it has not been recovered, and is
   never mentioned by the document whose central question it would answer** (§4).
4. **Two of the analytical documents contradict each other on whether forensic dating has been
   performed at all** (§6.2).

### 1.7 `Computational_Complexity_of_UAP_Reverse_Engineering.txt` (638 lines)

**Not by the archive.** A third-party paper by an author at a German foundation, published in a
named journal in March 2025 (received 21 Nov 2024, revised 7 Mar, accepted 13 Mar — six days from
revision to acceptance).

**It makes no claim about the manuscripts.** Searched the full text for the author's name, CARI,
Blackgrove, ASIRP, the editor's name, Sary-Shagan, RKIIGA, Riga, and anything Soviet: no hits. **It
does not argue that the manuscripts are authentic, and does not argue that anything is authentic
because reverse-engineering is hard.** Any connection to the archive's case is supplied entirely by
the framing around it.

**Is it complexity theory or complexity vocabulary?** One real theorem is cited correctly and then
applied in a way that does not survive its own preconditions.

- *Correct:* Gold 1978 (*Information and Control* 37(3):302–320) is real and its theorem is stated
  accurately: determining whether a finite automaton with a specified number of states agrees with
  a data set is NP-complete. The minimal-vs-minimum distinction is genuine and correctly drawn.
- *The central defect:* Gold's theorem holds under conditions the paper itself states — no holes in
  the characterization matrix, prefix-complete T, suffix-complete E (256–58). The paper then asserts
  repeatedly that the UAP instance **violates exactly those conditions** ("numerous gaps (holes),"
  427–28) and treats the violation as *escalating* the hardness (331, 349–52, 415–16). When a
  theorem's precondition fails you lose the theorem; you do not inherit a stronger one. That
  inversion is the load-bearing move of the paper.
- NP-completeness and undecidability are asserted in the same sentence (31–32, 452–53, 474–75).
  If the problem reduces to the Halting Problem it is not in NP and therefore not NP-complete.
- "Entscheidungsproblem" is used as though it were a complexity class ("escalate to… an
  Entscheidungsproblem"). It is a specific historical decision problem.
- PSPACE-hardness is described as membership rather than a lower bound (395–97).
- **No problem is ever defined**: no instance encoding, no input alphabet, no yes-instance, no size
  parameter, no reduction, no correctness proof.
- Of 29 numbered references, eleven are Wikipedia, one is a video, one is the author's own
  foundation website (the sole support for the technology the conclusion recommends), and one is
  an unnumbered final entry citing a commercial LLM product.

**It does function as an unfalsifiability shield**, independent of the corpus. §5.3 constructs the
strongest evidence anyone could ask for — a public, fully reproducible demonstration of every UAP
observable — and rules in advance that it would settle nothing (441–60). The conclusion generalises
this: no data-gathering route, passive observation *or* artifacts in hand, can succeed (480–83). It
then argues that classifying UAP technology is "fundamentally absurd" (485–87), which neutralises
secrecy as an explanation for why no corroborating record exists, and terminates in a
recommendation for the author's own foundation's technology (500–13).

### 1.9 `preprint1_…` and `preprint2_…`

**Neither is a published preprint.** Both are dated 18 May 2026 and carry the string "Preprint ·
Not peer-reviewed" and nothing else: **no arXiv identifier, no DOI, no venue, no repository, no

**Identity flag:** both preprints give the author's middle initial as "T." while all twelve critical

**preprint1 (superconductor valence survey)** claims GCD predicts T_c clustering at valence counts
Z = 3, 5, 7, and reports it confirmed on 31 elemental superconductors (p = 0.002, d = 0.89). The
statistics are competently executed and the limitations section is candid. But **the paper's own
Table 3 contradicts its headline**: Z = 3 has mean T_c 1.947 K, *below* Z = 4's 2.238 K. Z = 3 is
the only part of the prediction that goes beyond the 1955 Matthias rules — the paper says so — so
the novel component fails in the author's own data. The significance is carried by Z = 5 (Nb) and
Z = 7 (Tc) topping the elemental list, which is the Matthias result, published 71 years earlier.
Bundling two retrodicted peaks with one failed novel peak into a single 13-vs-18 test manufactures
the p-value; the informative test (Z = 3 alone) is not run. "Three independent statistical tests" is
wrong — three tests on one dataset are three estimators of one quantity. The paper concedes valence
assignment is non-unique and that "alternative assignments could shift individual elements between
groups," then does not run the sensitivity analysis; at n = 13/18, moving two or three elements
would erase the result. And the prediction's priority is not demonstrable: its sole source is a 2024
compilation from the same organisation, described in the same paper as both "published" and
"unpublished."

**preprint2 (revised planetary fields)** is considerably stronger, and largely negative about its
own subject. It states plainly that the framework's charge ratio makes F_Coulomb/F_grav = 1.233 for
*any* pair of bodies — the net Sun–planet force is repulsive by 23% — and that the required induced
field conflicts with measurement by ~10¹². It reports Venus and Mars predicted to have fields where
none exist, the giants over by 20–230×, and the Sun off by thousands. The arithmetic checks out.
Two defects: the one advertised success (Mercury to 7% "without any body-specific adjustment")
silently uses three author-chosen composition-class constants absent from the source; and Blackett's
1947 spinning-charged-body paper is cited but never engaged, so a 2026 revival of that hypothesis
has a gap where its literature review should be — Blackett's own 1952 null result goes unmentioned.

**Its value to this audit is inadvertent and considerable:** it establishes from the inside that
every planetary magnetic-field value in the manuscripts is a derived output of one Earth-calibrated
proportionality, not an observation. See §3.5.

## 2. The Period Plausibility Analysis: methodology, and the could/did distinction

### 2.1 Method

Three career windows; four questions asked of each technical claim (was the concept available; were
the parameters within reach; does the integration require post-window knowledge; does anything
contradict the window's record). Claims are "binned" against the documented research record.

### 2.2 COULD or DID?

**It supports COULD, it says so explicitly and repeatedly, and on the whole it does not conflate the
two.** That should be said first:

- L16–17: "The test runs a necessary condition for insider authenticity. It does not run a
  sufficient one."
- L22–25: passing "rules out amateur fabrication… It does not by itself categorically rule out
  sophisticated fabrication."
- L619: "supports the seriousness of the case. It does not settle the case."

**Where the conflation nonetheless enters**, at three specific joints:

1. **The absence-to-authenticity slide (L463–67).** "Every absence is consistent with the dating the
   corpus claims for itself. There are no anachronistic admissions." Consistency with a claimed date
   is not evidence *for* it unless the alternative would have produced inconsistency at a stated
   rate. The PPA never states a detection floor: it does not ask how many anachronisms a competent
   modern composer working from period sources would be expected to leak, nor over how many pages.
   Without that, "no anachronisms found" and "no anachronisms present" are not distinguished. This
   is the same failure mode the repo documents for negative image findings — and §3 below shows how
   much stronger the result becomes when the floor *is* stated.

2. **"Rules out amateur fabrication" (L22, L590–94)** is stated as a finding and supported only by
   the assertion that a fabricator "would face severe difficulties." No base rate, no control, no
   comparable corpus that failed the test.

3. **The transfer of weight to anchors it never inspects (L25–29, L599–603).** "The pressure on
   sophisticated-fabrication scenarios comes from the documentary anchors… particularly the August
   2012 my.mail.ru deposition… and the Sary-Shagan family residency record." The document that runs
   the plausibility test hands the actual dating work to two artifacts it does not examine. See §4.

**Verdict:** the PPA establishes that the corpus's *content* is consistent with the claimed period.
It does not establish, and mostly does not claim, that the corpus *was* written then. Its framing is
sound; the slippage is at the margins and in the summary, not in the design.

## 3. Anachronism check

### 3.1 Headline

**No numerical physical constant in the manuscript layer of any of the twelve works matches a
post-2000 revision against its pre-1990 value.** Where the two diverge, the manuscript carries the
older figure — in several cases conspicuously so. One dated hit exists and it is a 2024 self-dating,
not a leaked modern value (§3.6).

### 3.2 Hits

| # | Item | Location | Layer | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** | «Сегодня в 2024 году мы перейдём точку 6» — "**Today in 2024** we will pass point 6" | W10 p.4 (W10:592, 619, 664) | **manuscript** | **strong** |
| H2 | «2001 г. — приход Путина В.В. вместо Ельцина Б.Н.»; «2004 г. — СССР — ПЕРИОД РАСПАДА /до 2007/… США — ПЕРИОД УПАДКА /до 2005 г./» | W06:311/348; 520–23/559–62 | manuscript | strong (self-dating to ≈2004) |
| H3 | Mössbauer clock experiments dated to "В 1970 годы" at Harvard | W01:129, 137/183, 191 | manuscript | strong, **as an error** |
| H4 | R₀ = 8.5 kpc presented under the heading «АСТРОНОМИЧЕСКИЕ ХАРАКТЕРИСТИКИ **на 1966 год**» | W05:789/809; W12:754/791, 948/996, 956/976 | manuscript | weak as anachronism, **strong as internal contradiction** |
| H5 | "plasma actuators for boundary-layer control of supersonic aircraft" | W01:1191–92 | **undecidable** | ambiguous |

**H1 is the significant one.** Work 10 page 1 uses the future tense ("in 2024 the Solar System
**will pass**"), which is writable at any earlier date. Page 4 uses «Сегодня» — *today*. That places
the writing-present of that page at ≈2024, **outside both claimed composition windows** (1980–2007
and 2004–2019 alike, the latter only just). It is not evidence of leaked modern knowledge; it is
evidence that at least one page of the corpus is late. Whether it is a late addition to an older
corpus, a copying date, or something else is not decidable from the text.

**H3 points backwards, not forwards.** Pound–Rebka is 1959, Pound–Snider 1965. The manuscript also

### 3.8 Detection floors — where an anachronism could hide from this audit

Stating these is the difference between a null that means something and one that does not.

- **Figures.** A large share of manuscript content in every work exists only as a bracketed
  editorial summary (`[Diagram: …]`). Works 5 pp. 6, 8, 9; Work 6 pp. 1, 3; Work 7 pp. 1, 5, 6;
  Work 8 p. 2 are substantially or wholly diagrammatic. **Any numeral, annotation, date or term
  written inside a figure is invisible to this audit.**
- **Pages with no transcription at all.** Work 8 pp. 2, 5 and 6 of the main body (W08:228–253) have
  no Russian transcription and no English translation — roughly **half of Work 8's main body exists
  solely as editorial paraphrase**. Work 1 p. 12 is likewise omitted, which is why H5 is
  undecidable.
- **No page images in the editions.** Stamps, letterheads, handwriting changes, ink and paper
  evidence, and marginalia are outside the text corpus entirely. The date-stamp null in §3.5 is the
  weakest of the nulls for exactly this reason — a marginal date is precisely the kind of thing
  rendered as an unremarked pen-stroke and dropped from a transcription, and none of the editions
  claims to record marginalia.
- **Editorial labels are not perfectly reliable.** H4 documents one case where the editor asserts
  something incorrect about the manuscript's sourcing. If a value were interpolated into a block
  labelled "Russian transcription," text alone would not reveal it.
- **Elided lists.** The ФЭС entry list terminates in «и т.д.» twice; the full set of entries drawn on
  is not enumerated.
- **The corpus was not fully audited by transcription.** The Ball Lightning paper (PDF pp. 2–4) —
  the source of the single most-quoted figure in the entire media campaign — **has no critical
  edition**; W08 CE:1026 records it as "not yet examined."

### 3.9 Two findings the editions do not flag

Both are checkable and both bear on how the corpus was assembled.

**(a) Work 11 page 3: the Saturn and Uranus I_center cells are transposed.** Recomputing each row's

### 5.3 The 16.65 derivation — resolved

The ACH designates as "the single highest-priority action" (L1245–48) verifying where
H_Sun(max) = 1.66×10⁵ Oe came from, and makes Terminus Condition A depend on it. The archive's own
Work 08 critical edition already contains the full chain (W08 CE:51–58, 632, 796–800):

```
H_Sun_max = (q_Sun / q_Earth) · H_Earth = (1.9·10²⁰ / 5.72·10¹⁴) · 0.5 Oe = 1.66·10⁵ Oe
B_Sun_max = μ₀ · 79.6 · H_Sun_max = 1.26·10⁻⁶ · 79.6 · 1.66·10⁵ = 16.65 T = 16.65 kg/(coul·sec)
I = B / (μ·μ₀·N/L) = 16.65 / (1 · 1.26·10⁻⁶ · 8000) = 1.65·10³ A
```

Verified: 1.9×10²⁰/5.72×10¹⁴ = **3.32×10⁵**, which is the **Sun/Earth mass ratio** (M☉/M⊕ =
3.33×10⁵). H_Earth = 0.5 Oe is Earth's surface field. So:

> **16.65 T = 0.5 Oe × 332,000, restated in SI.**

Three consequences, none of which the ACH draws:

1. **1.66×10⁵ Oe is not quoted from any literature.** It is computed from two figures available in
   any textbook since the nineteenth century. The ACH's Priority 1 — "survey Soviet-era astrophysics
   literature for a published value of 1.66×10⁵ Oe" — is looking for something that was never
   claimed to be a literature value. As posed, the question cannot resolve either way.
2. **The Oersted-to-Tesla step is the identity 1 Oe = 10⁻⁴ T**, a single multiplication. The ACH
   calls it "a multi-step Oersteds-to-Tesla derivation" (L881) and rests the defeat of the
   AI-translation contamination pathway on its multi-step character. It is one step.
3. **The triad is one number, not three.** W08 CE:58 states it outright: "The 32-solenoid / 16.65
   Tesla / 1.65 kA triad is therefore **not three independent specifications** but a single
   derivation chain ending on page 15." And W08 CE:804: 32 is "the round number that closes the
   calculation at 1.65·10³ A." Yet the ACH lists B_max, solenoid count and operating current as
   three separate EXACT specifications (L364–71), and both the ACH and the Rosetta Stone treat their
   recurrence on three pages of one corpus as corroborating convergence. W08 CE:820 itself slides
   here, describing "convergence… across three independent presentations," using "independent" in a
   different sense 760 lines after denying it in the first.

### 5.4 Hypotheses omitted

The five hypotheses cover *how the convergence arose* given the corpus. **None is a hypothesis about
composition other than binary authenticity.** Specifically absent:

- **Later interpolation into a genuine older corpus** — that some pages are period and others are
  not. Given a physically heterogeneous corpus (pink paper, typescript, three ink colours) whose
  Work 6 self-dates to 2004 and whose Work 10 p.4 self-dates to **2024**, this is the most natural
  intermediate hypothesis available and the one a page-by-page forensic programme would test. It is
  nowhere in the five.
- **Editorial construction** — that the *specifications* as inventoried are artifacts of extraction
  rather than of the source pages. The ACH's own inventory demonstrates the risk: it lists "Primary
  fusion reaction **Cl + ⁶Li → 2⁴He**, EXACT" (L381) with no flag, when mass numbers do not balance
  and the PPA flags it as corresponding to no documented reaction; and "Electron density at ignition
  **> 1.2×10¹² /m³**" where the Ball Lightning facsimile shows `E < 0,12·10¹³`. Reaction chemistry
  and inequality direction are the two things an extraction pipeline is most likely to corrupt, and
  both are recorded as EXACT. Work 11's transposed table rows (§3.9a) are the same failure mode
  caught in the transcription itself.
- **Selection effects in the reader.** The corpus contains hundreds of numbers; the analysis reports
  those that matched. No accounting of the non-matching remainder appears anywhere.

### 5.5 So: a brief in ACH clothing?

No — not honestly characterised that way. It reaches "no claim has achieved Tier I," reports a null
on its own Pais comparator, engages VINITI as a live undefeated threat, and declines an
unsupportable Bayesian calculation. A one-sided brief does not do those things.

But its lapses are not randomly distributed: the comparator chosen for matching produced the
headline result; the three untested comparators were three chances at a non-correspondence; the
"multi-step derivation" characterisation that defeats a contamination pathway overstates a one-step
conversion; and the triad counted as three specifications is one. **The methodology is real, and
every place it slips, it slips the same way.**

## 6. The Rosetta Stone

### 6.1 What it claims to decode, and with what key

The "Rosetta Stone" is **the manuscript corpus itself**, and what it claims to decode is **the
portfolio of US government research programmes**. The claimed key is the *integrated architecture*:
taken as a reference template, seven manuscript subsystems (MHD propulsion, aneutronic fusion,
superconducting solenoids, vacuum/metric engineering, charge–gravity coupling, EM field
generator/disc craft, advanced hull materials) are mapped onto named programmes and funding lines
(DARPA PUMP, ARPA-E WHAM, DIRDs #1/2/4/5/9/14/15/30/33/37, Pais patents, Eagleworks, Lockheed CFR),
totalling a claimed \$1.2B+.

The thesis is at L1973–77: "Without Černohajev's integrated architecture as a decoder key, these
programs appear to be independent, unrelated research efforts. With it, they resolve into a
systematic, subsystem-by-subsystem investigation of a single propulsion concept."

Note what the analogy does and does not license. The Rosetta Stone worked because it carried the
*same text* in a known and an unknown script, and the decipherment was checkable against further
texts. Here there is no parallel text and no independent check: the "decoding" is a one-directional
mapping from a template onto programmes that are already public and already described in their own
terms. A template broad enough to cover seven subsystems of speculative propulsion will map onto a
speculative-propulsion research portfolio. **The document supplies no control** — no attempt to map
an unrelated template onto the same programme set and show that it fails.

### 6.2 The forensic claim

`Rosetta Stone:2242`, in the hypothesis table, rejects the fabrication hypothesis as:

> "Contradicted by forensic evidence: 85–95% authentication confidence for period-appropriate
> materials, single authorship, Soviet scientific conventions."

**This figure appears exactly once in the entire body of material, with no source, no laboratory, no
examiner, no method and no date.** Three months earlier the ACH — same principal, same organisation
— lists forensic paper and ink analysis as **Priority 2, not yet performed** (ACH:1154–59), states
"Forensic paper and ink analysis of the physical documents remains required for full independence
verification" (ACH:491–93), and makes a post-2007 forensic date a terminus condition. The Dossier
likewise has it as future work in the subjunctive: "If the manuscripts *can be* dated…"
(`Dossier:1688–90`). The evidence map recommends FOIA work precisely because no Soviet-side
documentation has been obtained.

An 85–95% authentication confidence cannot coexist with three sibling documents saying the analysis
has not been run. This is the sharpest internal contradiction in the material, and it sits on the
load-bearing claim.

### 6.3 The joint probability

Stated four different ways in one document:

| Value | Location |
|---|---|
| < 0.05% | L48 (At a Glance) |
| < 0.1% | L167 (Executive Summary) |
| 2.4 × 10⁻¹² | L1598 (the calculation) |
| < 10⁻⁴ = 0.01% | L1601, L1613 (stat box, after clustering) |

Beyond the inconsistency, the method has a specific defect. L1583–85: "If the seventeen convergence
points are treated as conditionally independent events (**a conservative assumption** — some are
likely correlated)…" Treating correlated events as independent is not conservative; it is the
assumption that maximally *inflates* the result. The document identifies the correlation and then
labels the inflating assumption as the cautious one.

The seventeen CP values (8%–35%) have no stated derivation, no reference class and no method. At
least three pairs are the same fact entered twice (CP-02/CP-13 are both Li-Al-B/Z=3; CP-03/CP-11 are
both DIRD #33; CP-07/CP-15 are both the same two researchers). And CP-01 — the 8% that dominates the
product — is the 16.65 T value, which §5.3 shows is a unit restatement of Earth's field times the
mass ratio.

### 6.4 Other internal problems

- **Hypothesis-table probabilities do not sum**: H1 35–45%, H2 25–35%, H3 5–10%, H4 2–5% — 67–95%
  across an exhaustive set.
- **H1 is credited for "Consistent with all evidence."** A hypothesis consistent with all evidence
  has no discriminating power; in ACH terms that is grounds to demote it, not promote it.
- **Same numeral, two dimensions, called self-consistency.** L480: "7,150 km/sec arm speed… Self-
  consistent with Bsun = 7,150 Gauss." A speed and a magnetic field cannot be self-consistent; they
  share a digit string. (And §3.9b suggests the 7,150 chain traces to a number already in the
  author's own table.)
- **Birth decade**: "(1940s–2019)" (L67) vs. the ACH's and Biographical Foundation's 1958.
- **Provenance of the headline number**: CP-01 (L1122) attributes the 16.65 T derivation to "Works
  №7 and №11"; the ACH sources it to "Work No. 2 (VC-003)" (L366); the critical editions place the
  closed-form derivation in Work No. 8's addendum.
- **WHAM date**: CP-01 says "WHAM (2020)"; the ACH and media kit say July 2024.
- **AAWSAP date**: L2243 says manuscripts "predate AAWSAP (2007)"; the ACH's own reference [14]
  dates the contract to 2008.
- **It contradicts its sibling on its own flagship point.** The Rosetta Stone and Dossier both state
  "Z=3 was not a traditional Matthias peak" — the entire basis for treating Li-Al-B as a
  *non-canonical* prospective prediction. The evidence map states the opposite: Matthias found T_c
  peaks "at valence electron counts Z=3, 5, and 7 — an exact match." And preprint1's own Table 3
  (§1.9) shows Z=3 underperforming Z=4 in the data. Three documents from one shop, three positions.

## 7. The podcast prep kit and social clip scripts

**Characterisation, fairly.** These are competent, professionally structured media assets: a
three-minute pitch, twenty pull-quotes with delivery notes, five "revelation moments," hostile-
question preparation, a one-page host briefing, and ten platform-specific 45–60 second scripts. The
prep kit is marked "CONFIDENTIAL" and "NOT FOR PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION WITHOUT PERMISSION"; the clips
are production briefs, not published claims. They should be weighed as internal working documents.

**What they tell you about intended reception.** Three things, and they are not all discreditable.

**First, the archive wants the material received as falsifiable science, and it says so
consistently.** The kit's stated close is a test programme, not a belief: "The most important thing
about these manuscripts is that they're falsifiable"; "The framework is designed to be falsified,
not believed"; the host briefing's "What Gene won't do: Claim certainty." Six named experiments with
costed budgets and stated decisive outcomes is a real commitment, and the refrain "the experiments
have not been run" is an honest statement of where the case stands. The hostile-question answers
engage the strongest objections (Western copying, free energy, the astrology charge) rather than
dodging them, and the civilizational-cycle answer explicitly separates "the strong claims from the
speculative claims."

**Second, the epistemic register hardens as it moves outward from the scholarship.** The same claims
are stated with progressively less hedging the further they get from the critical editions:

| Claim | Critical editions / ACH / PPA | Media layer |
|---|---|---|
| The 16.65 value | facsimile: `B = <16,65`, an upper bound; PPA: units "require reconciliation" | "a magnetic confinement field of **16.65 Tesla for the reactor**"; "the magnetic confinement field **required**" |
| Physical form | editions describe typed, handwritten and mixed pages accurately | "**119 handwritten pages**"; "In blue and red ink. **On grid paper**" — the scans show pink paper, white typescript and three ink colours |
| The 2024 prediction | W06 CE:778: "**The 2024 prediction did not bear out**… will mark Work No. 6's principal forward prediction as **falsified**" | Revelation 5: "He wrote that decades before the current disclosure movement existed… **The timing matches**" |
| Forensics | ACH: not performed, Priority 2 | Rosetta Stone: "85–95% authentication confidence" |
| The ACH's own status | ACH: "DOI: [pending]"; "arXiv preprint version: [to be assigned upon submission]" | "We published the full analysis **with a DOI**" |
| Independent derivation | W08 CE: 16.65 T / 32 solenoids / 1.65 kA are "not three independent specifications" | "Three is a pattern" |

The Work 6 case is the clearest. The archive's own scholarly layer records the corpus's single
precisely-dated forward prediction as failed, and says explicitly that this "should be recorded
honestly in any external presentation of the corpus's claims." The external presentation does the
opposite.

**Third, the recurring framing device is inference-by-adjacency.** "Governments don't imprison
people for studying things that don't work" (Bite 12, Revelation 3, Clip 08) converts a prosecution
into a validation. "One match is coincidence. Two is interesting. Three is a pattern" (Clip 03) is
stated with no base rate. "Connect the dots" (Clip 08) instructs the viewer to supply the inference
the material does not make.

**Relevance to weighing the other documents.** Two of the analytical documents — the Rosetta Stone
and the ACH — are cited *by* the media layer as its credentials ("the same methodology the
intelligence community uses… published with a DOI… name another UAP claim that does that"). The
Rosetta Stone in turn closes with recommendations to Congress. The analytical and promotional layers
are not independent products: same principal, mutual citation, and the promotional layer is where
the analytical layer's caveats are dropped. That is the relevant fact for weighting, and it is a
fact about the corpus's internal structure, not a judgement about anyone's motives.

## 8. Structural observations about the archive as a whole

1. **The organisations are not independent.** CARI, ASIRP and Blackgrove Global Risk trace to one
   principal: the ORCID on the ACH title page is the same ORCID on every critical edition's "Editor"
   line and on both preprints. The Rosetta Stone's footers carry Blackgrove and ASIRP identifiers on
   alternating pages, and the header records a rebranding. The PPA calls itself "the third
   foundational reference… alongside the biographical foundation and the technical correlation
   analysis" — three pillars from one shop. The Dossier consolidates eight prior products, **all
   eight in-house**.
2. **The evidence map is inside the dossier, and the paste is visible.** The evidence map (May 2026)
   says an arXiv paper was "published just four months ago" (Jan→May, correct); the Dossier (July
   2026) says "published four months before this assessment" (Jan→Jul, six months). So the Dossier's
   "Evidence Base" chapter is an in-house document, commissioned by the publishing side, prepared by
   a disclosed AI system, re-presented as an independent evidentiary layer.
3. **The two independent-looking technical documents are not about the corpus at all.** Both the
   complexity paper and the Pais-patents breakdown contain **no reference to the manuscripts, the
   author, or the archive**. Whatever work they do in the case is done by the framing around them.
4. **The critical editions are the most reliable layer.** Across every check run, the critical-
   edition apparatus was accurate, self-correcting and willing to record findings against interest:
   it flags the author's Mössbauer dating error; notes his galactic field value is eight orders of
   magnitude high; records that the 16.65 T solar field exceeds measurement by orders of magnitude
   and is "the GCD prediction, not a measured value"; states that the triad is one derivation rather
   than three; records the 2024 prediction as falsified; concedes the Dictionary's reaction table
   cannot be cross-checked. Where the derived documents disagree with the critical editions, the
   critical editions were right every time it was checkable. Two exceptions are documented at §3.9
   and H4 — the editions are the best layer, not an infallible one.

## 10. What these documents CAN and CANNOT establish

### They CAN establish

1. **That the corpus's content is period-consistent, and the null is unusually strong.** Three
   independent sweeps found no numerical constant in the manuscript layer matching a post-2000
   revision. Where era and modern values diverge, the manuscript carries the era value: G at the
   CODATA-1973 figure matching the cited 1983 dictionary, with M_⊕ and M_☉ cohering with *that* G;
   a 30 eV neutrino bound; a nine-planet table whose Uranus rotation period, Neptune radius and
   Pluto mass place its source before 1974–78 and exclude every relevant spacecraft encounter. The
   terminology is clean of every post-1990 tripwire searched. The superconductor discussion is
   pre-1986 Matthias framing with no cuprates. The nuclear model is pre-1932. The units are
   consistently SI-with-Soviet-conventions with no CGS leakage and a genre-appropriate register
   switch. The external citations are few, real and period-appropriate. **The strongest single
   element is that the errors propagate correctly** — the density column is wrong in exactly the way
   its own era radii require, and the derived planetary fields contradict measurements public since
   1965–67, which is harder to construct than the values themselves.
2. **That the corpus's technical content was within reach of a Soviet-trained engineer of the
   claimed era.** The PPA's survey of the research record is accurate where checked.
3. **That the corpus is internally coherent as engineering documentation.** The specifications
   reconcile with each other (16.65 T → 1.65 kA given the stated geometry; 678 m² × 46 t/m² →
   ~31,000 t thrust; the charge density and coulomb figure are one quantity in two units).
4. **That named US programmes exist with the parameters described**, and that a mapping between them
   and the corpus's subsystems can be drawn.

### They CANNOT establish

1. **When the manuscripts were physically produced.** No forensic dating has been performed — stated
   as such by the ACH, the Dossier and the evidence map. The one document claiming a forensic result
   (85–95%) contradicts all three and cites nothing. No manuscript page carries an absolute date
   stamp. The corpus's own internal self-dating gives **2004** in Work 6 and **2024** in Work 10
   page 4 — the latter outside both claimed composition windows.
2. **Who wrote them, or where that person worked.** No educational record, employment record,
   security-clearance record, residency permit, birth or death record, or archival reference is
   produced anywhere in the material. Both institutional placements are undocumented, each appears
   in only half the archive, and the one asserted as "confirmed" four times traces to a single
   self-report in a comment thread. With ~7.5 years of the author's own quoted words available,
   none of them mentions either institution, and his one self-description does not match the
   constructed career.
3. **That the content is older than its period-consistency implies.** Period-consistent content is
   compatible with composition at any later date by anyone working from period sources. The PPA
   concedes this for sophisticated fabrication and transfers the weight to anchors it never
   inspects. Without a detection floor — how many anachronisms a competent period-sourced
   composition would leak, over how many pages — "no anachronisms found" does not convert into
   "written then." And this audit's own floors (§3.8) are real: figures are invisible, roughly half
   of Work 8's main body has no transcription, and the single most-quoted page in the campaign has
   no critical edition at all.
4. **That the convergences are anomalous.** The number carrying the case, 16.65 T, is Earth's
   surface field times the Sun/Earth mass ratio, in SI. Both inputs were available in any era. The
   ACH's own base-rate control finds an independent 15.8 T inside its threshold band. The comparator
   that produced the headline match was selected for matching. Three of eight pre-registered
   comparators were never tested. The joint probability rests on independence the documents
   themselves deny, and three of its seventeen inputs are duplicates.
5. **That the corpus's physics works.** Not the question here, but the archive's own preprint2
   reports the framework makes the Sun–planet force net repulsive by 23%, misses the giant planets
   by 20–230×, predicts fields on Venus and Mars where none exist, and requires induced fields
   conflicting with measurement by ~10¹². The archive is more candid about this than its media layer
   suggests.
6. **Anything about the video footage this repo investigates.** Nothing in this material touches it.
   Whoever connected the two produced no link, and none is visible here.

### The two things that would change this

**A locator for the August 2012 posting** — a thread URL, an archive capture, the numbered
photographs. If dated 2012 images of these pages exist in a public archive, that is a hard upper
bound on their production date for those pages and it would do more work than every other document
in this set combined. The Biographical Foundation quotes the thread at length and states in the same
breath that recovering it is future work; the ACH does not mention it at all.

**Page-level forensic dating**, which the ACH itself makes Priority 2. Given a physically
heterogeneous corpus with self-dating markers 20 years apart, the useful question is not "is it
period or not" but "which pages are period" — and that is the hypothesis the ACH's five do not
contain.

### Net weight

**These provenance arguments can carry the claim that the corpus reads as period-appropriate, and
that claim is better supported than I expected going in. They cannot carry the claim that it is
period-produced, and they do not establish authorship or placement at all.** The critical-edition
layer is careful and worth reading; the analytical layer is mixed, with the ACH substantially better
than the Rosetta Stone; the promotional layer states as settled several things the archive's own
scholarship records as open, as unperformed, or as falsified. Provenance remains undetermined.
